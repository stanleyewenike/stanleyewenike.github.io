Reporting readiness assurance for 15 Health Innovation Networks

Organisation
NHS England
Role
Evaluation Senior Manager and Analytics Lead
Period
2025 to 2026

I designed and ran an assurance exercise to test whether each of the 15 Health Innovation Networks could deliver a national quarterly dataset within a shorter reporting window. I then reported their readiness to the programme's Steering and governance group.

Situation

Reporting was moving to a shorter window. The networks use different systems and processes, some were changing systems mid-quarter, and stakeholders were worried about reporting burden.

Task

Design a proportionate assurance instrument, collect returns from every network, and turn them into a readiness assessment with recommendations.

Action

  • Drafted a 22-question template covering data quality, timeliness, system configuration and milestones.
  • Consulted stakeholders in several rounds, and cut the template to a one-off 6-question snapshot to keep the burden low.
  • Analysed the returns by timeliness against target, risk themes and organisational maturity.
  • Summarised the position with a SWOT analysis and prioritised the recommendations using MoSCoW.

Result

  • All 15 networks returned the snapshot.
  • The Steering and governance group received an evidence-based readiness assessment, with prioritised recommendations, which informed the decisions to move to the shorter window and later to keep it.

Skills and tools

Assurance framework design, template and survey design, stakeholder consultation, qualitative synthesis, maturity modelling, SWOT, MoSCoW, report writing for senior governance.

What I learnt

  • A short instrument that everyone completes beats a thorough one that only some complete.
  • An average against a target can hide a wide spread, so report the distribution.
  • Clearly prioritised recommendations get acted on.
