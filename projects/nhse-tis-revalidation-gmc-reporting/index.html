Revalidation submission dashboard and GMC reference data quality report

Organisation
NHS England
Role
Data Analyst
Period
2023 to 2024

I built two Tableau products on the national trainee information system, which holds the training records of doctors in postgraduate training. One measures the completeness and validity of doctors' GMC reference numbers. The other tracks revalidation recommendations and whether they reach the GMC on time.

Situation

Revalidation depends on each doctor's GMC reference number and on recommendations reaching the GMC by the due date. Missing or invalid GMC references break that link, and revalidation teams worked to a KPI for on-time submission.

Task

Build a data quality report for GMC references, and a dashboard that helps revalidation teams track submissions.

Action

  • Built the data quality report on a live, published data source: completeness and validity measures by local office, flag categories for missing and invalid references, and row-level details for correction. I did not report accuracy, because the data lacked the parameters to measure it.
  • Built the revalidation dashboard by blending the revalidation system's exports with live training data, with academic-year and year-to-date views, year-on-year comparisons, and submission punctuality against GMC due dates by recommendation status, outcome and type.
  • The dashboard was reviewed with revalidation colleagues as a prototype, and their feedback was recorded.
  • Wrote step-by-step publishing guidance for the reports, so others could maintain and republish them.

Result

I published the data quality report in 2023, and GMC reference data quality improved. After the prototype review, the revalidation dashboard went live for national and regional revalidation teams and programme administrators.

Skills and tools

Tableau (published data sources, data blending, level-of-detail expressions, parameters), data quality measurement, KPI design, prototyping with users.

What I learnt

  • A data quality report earns trust by saying what it cannot measure.
  • A dashboard for a KPI should show who still needs action, not just the rate.
  • Engage the people who own the process before showing a prototype more widely.
