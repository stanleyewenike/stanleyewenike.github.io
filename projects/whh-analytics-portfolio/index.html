Covid-era analytics across clinical systems

Organisation
Warrington and Halton Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust
Role
Principal Information Analyst
Period
2021 to 2022

During the Covid period I delivered SQL analyses that joined several clinical systems. They supported regional vaccination monitoring, Covid treatment and discharge cohorts, perioperative care for older people, patient flow and maternity data quality.

Situation

Clinical teams, operations and regional partners raised new questions at short notice, and answering them meant joining several hospital systems.

Task

Deliver accurate, documented extracts and analyses for each request, often on a recurring schedule.

Action

  • Built a weekly register of pregnant patients for the regional population health platform (CIPHA) to support vaccine uptake work.
  • Defined cohorts of patients discharged with Covid, and of patients receiving Covid antibody and antiviral treatments. These joined pathology, order communications, electronic prescribing, bed management and patient administration data.
  • Tracked "right to reside" status from bed history, using window functions to group repeated states.
  • Analysed surgical liaison contacts for older patients, with length of stay and 30-day readmissions.
  • Answered a Freedom of Information request, and ran maternity data quality checks, including validation of swab dates and maintenance of BadgerNet records.
  • Used a standard script header recording the request, data period and output for every job.

Result

Recurring and one-off outputs supported regional vaccination monitoring, Covid treatment pathways, patient flow management and statutory responses.

Skills and tools

T-SQL (multi-system joins, window functions, cohort definitions), Lorenzo, ICE, ePMA, pathology and BadgerNet data, regional data sharing, Freedom of Information responses.

What I learnt

  • A standard script header is the cheapest documentation there is, and it still works years later.
  • Knowing every source system, not just the warehouse, made fast responses possible.
  • Record the volume and use of each output when you deliver it.
