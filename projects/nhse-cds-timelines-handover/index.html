Evidence and delivery for a shorter national reporting window

Organisation
NHS England
Role
Evaluation Senior Manager and Analytics Lead
Period
2025 to 2026

I provided the evidence and analysis that informed the decision to shorten the time between quarter end and assurance for a national dataset submitted by the 15 Health Innovation Networks. I oversaw the delivery of the supporting reporting products, and handed the programme over to another team.

Situation

Data collection closed six weeks after quarter end, and assurance reached commissioners two weeks after that. This was too late for in-year decisions.

Task

Make a shorter reporting window workable, oversee delivery of the reporting products, and hand the programme over cleanly.

Action

  • Provided readiness evidence and analysis for the shorter window.
  • Maintained the quarterly reporting and assurance timetables.
  • Oversaw the work packages for the assurance dashboard, the website and ongoing maintenance.
  • Designed a four-stage data quality framework for submissions.
  • Automated the production of the quarterly data packs in Python.
  • Produced a handover briefing, a transition deck and written handover notes.

Result

  • Senior management cut the reporting window from six weeks to three and later decided to keep it. My readiness evidence and analysis informed both decisions.
  • The programme was handed over with a full briefing pack, and I gave ad hoc support for six months afterwards to keep the transition smooth.

Skills and tools

Programme and timeline management, supplier oversight, data quality governance, Python automation, stakeholder communication, structured handover.

What I learnt

  • Shortening a reporting window takes both a governance decision and the tooling to meet it.
  • Work packages need clear acceptance criteria from the start.
  • A good handover pack records the reasons behind decisions, not just the current state.
