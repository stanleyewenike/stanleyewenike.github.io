Patient appointment no-show analysis

Organisation
Personal project (technical assessment)
Role
Sole author
Period
2025 to 2026

An analysis of missed appointments by condition and GP practice, with relational and dimensional data models and Azure infrastructure-as-code templates for hosting.

Situation

A 5,000-record appointment dataset.

Task

Analyse no-show patterns, design the data models and show a deployable architecture.

Action

  • Calculated no-show rates by condition and by GP practice.
  • Designed relational and dimensional models for the appointment data.
  • Wrote Azure infrastructure-as-code templates for hosting.

Result

The analysis was delivered with data models and deployment templates, covering everything from analysis to hosting.

Skills and tools

Python, Jupyter, SQL, descriptive analysis, relational and dimensional modelling, Azure infrastructure as code.

What I learnt

  • Practice-level rates need confidence intervals before anyone compares practices.
  • Pairing analysis with a deployment design shows end-to-end thinking.
