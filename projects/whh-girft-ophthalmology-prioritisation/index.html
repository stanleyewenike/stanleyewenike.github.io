Ophthalmology follow-up prioritisation against the GIRFT standard

Organisation
Warrington and Halton Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust
Role
Principal Information Analyst
Period
2021

I built a T-SQL stored procedure and a Power BI report showing which ophthalmology follow-up patients were overdue against their Guaranteed Activity Date. The service used them to prioritise patients against the Getting It Right First Time (GIRFT) failsafe standard.

Situation

Follow-up patients across three ophthalmology services had to be seen within 25% of their Guaranteed Activity Date, against a 95% target. The service had no reliable view of who was overdue.

Task

Produce a refreshable view of performance against the standard, and a patient-level list for clinical prioritisation.

Action

  • Wrote a stored procedure joining the outpatient waiting-list, appointment and offer data.
  • Used the later of the Guaranteed Activity Date and the patient's "do not offer before" date, so that patients were not shown as breaching when they had asked to be seen later.
  • Added data quality flags for removals with no next appointment, missing wait days and past-dated appointments. Flagged records were held apart rather than silently counted.
  • Built a Power BI report with a scheduled refresh.

Result

The service gained a monthly trend against the 95% target, an overdue summary and a patient-level breach list. These were used to prioritise patients and to pilot service changes.

Skills and tools

T-SQL (stored procedures, date logic), Power BI, outpatient waiting-list data, GIRFT standards, data quality flagging.

What I learnt

  • For waiting-list standards, the date rule is the analysis. Agree it with clinicians first.
  • Data quality flags give the admin team a work list rather than discarded records.
  • Capture a baseline before a service change, so the improvement can be shown.
