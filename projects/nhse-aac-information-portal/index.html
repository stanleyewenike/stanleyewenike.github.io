Research and innovation KPI dashboards

Organisation
NHS England (Accelerated Access Collaborative)
Role
Evaluation Senior Manager and Analytics Lead
Period
2024

I inherited two Tableau workbooks for the Accelerated Access Collaborative's information portal and extended them:

  • one tracks national clinical research delivery
  • the other tracks innovations that respond to NHS demand signals

Situation

A colleague and an external consultancy had built earlier versions of the dashboards, which then needed an owner inside the team.

Task

Take ownership of both workbooks, keep them current and extend them.

Action

  • Developed the research workbook to version 11. Its four dashboards cover seven KPIs, including study recruitment, Be Part of Research sign-ups (including through the NHS App), site set-up times and national priority trials.
  • Developed the demand-signalling workbook to version 11. It covers innovations linked to demand signals, funding, projects requested and completed, and the completeness of innovation records.
  • Kept the existing interaction design consistent: toggles, zoom and KPI tiles.

Result

Two maintained, extended workbooks ready for the portal, with ownership brought inside the team.

Skills and tools

Tableau (calculated fields, parameters, interactive design), KPI reporting, taking over and maintaining existing BI products, research delivery metrics.

What I learnt

  • Inheriting a BI product means inheriting its data pipeline, so map the sources first.
  • Heavy calculation logic in a workbook is a sign the data layer needs work.
  • Recorded handovers are valuable, and I now make them for my own work too.
