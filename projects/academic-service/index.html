Academic service: conference committee roles

Organisation
International computing conferences
Role
Publicity Chair, Publicity Co-Chair, Technical Program Committee member
Period
2015 to 2024

I have held 7 committee roles at international computing conferences in distributed systems, the Internet of Things and edge computing, mostly alongside full-time NHS work.

Situation

After my PhD research I moved into NHS analytics, which offers little routine contact with academic research.

Task

Stay active in the research community.

Action

  • Technical Program Committee member, IEEE ICC 2015 workshop (London).
  • Publicity Chair for AICCSA 2020, IOTSMS 2020, FMEC 2021, IOTSMS 2024 and FMEC 2024.
  • Publicity Co-Chair for SNAMS 2023.

Result

7 roles across 5 conference series over ten years, including repeat invitations from two of them.

Skills and tools

Research community engagement, conference promotion, peer review, event coordination.

What I learnt

  • Committee service is a low-cost way to stay visible in a research field.
  • Applied NHS work can feed back into research publications.
